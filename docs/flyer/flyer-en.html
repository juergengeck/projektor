In the browser · In minutes · With you

Ready in minutes

  • Open the browser — and you keep your own project record.
  • The browser you already have is all it takes.
  • Carry on with Outlook, your file server and the programs you already use.

Open and traceable

  • Methods and formats are published and documented.
  • For every entry you can see what it rests on: who stated it, when, and on what basis.
  • Where two records contradict each other, both sides are shown it.

Yours to keep

  • Your data sits in your browser, on your machine, under your control.
  • Your record stays yours — beyond the end of the project, and when a licence lapses.
  • You release deliberately: exactly the state, exactly to the recipient.

Sound for years

  • Every commitment carries the signature of whoever stated it — with its time and its basis.
  • Alter it afterwards and the signature shows it.
  • The check runs on your own machine, offline, and still works years later.
How the paper trail forms
1

Everyone keeps their own

Each office, authority and specialist planner holds their own project record.

2

Everyone signs what they commit to

Whoever states a commitment, an approval or a date signs it — and it reaches the others signed.

3

Everyone holds their proof

What reaches you carries the signature of whoever stated it. That makes every copy as sound as the original.
